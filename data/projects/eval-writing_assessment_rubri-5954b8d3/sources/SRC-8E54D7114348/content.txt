Learning outcomes:
- Use brainstorming processes to discover topics, positions, and supporting details.
- Apply recursive strategies for drafting, peer review, revision, rewriting, and editing.
- Compose a position argument that integrates the writer's ideas with appropriate sources.
- Give and act on productive feedback.
- Apply or challenge language and grammar conventions during revision.

Relevant content summary:
A position argument takes a stance on a debatable issue and supports it with reasoning and evidence. Writers should consider audience and purpose, integrate source material with their own ideas, and treat writing as a recursive rather than strictly linear process. Assessment should distinguish the quality of the thesis, reasoning, evidence integration, audience awareness, organization, revision, and language choices.
